Platform Overview

Structuring the unstructured workforce through vector intelligence.

Empowering the Unorganized Sector

blueHIRE bridges the gap between massive, unstructured blue-collar talent pools and the site managers who need them. By transforming raw paper applications and messy PDFs into mathematically searchable embeddings, we ensure no skilled worker is lost in the filing cabinet.

🎯

For Recruiters

Stop manually sorting resumes. Query the database using natural language (e.g., "welder with 5 years experience") and let the ML engine instantly identify top matches based on semantic skill meaning.

⚡

Bulk AI Parsing

Upload thousands of unstructured resumes at once in ZIP format. Our ephemeral processing pipeline reads, extracts, and vectorizes candidate data in temporary memory for high-speed, zero-storage ingestion.
